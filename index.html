Technology rarely changes everything overnight. More often, new ideas slowly become useful, then familiar, and eventually become part of everyday life.

The most interesting developments happen where technology meets human behavior. Better interfaces, smarter tools and new ways of working can create opportunities that were difficult to imagine only a few years earlier.

That is why understanding the direction of technology matters just as much as following individual product launches.
